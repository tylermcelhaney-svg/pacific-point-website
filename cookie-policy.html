Cookie Policy

How Pacific Point Partners uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Last Updated: 05/01/2026

Our Approach to Cookies

Pacific Point Partners uses a minimal cookie footprint. We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral tracking. This website is designed to function correctly without requiring your consent to non-essential cookies.

What Cookies We Use

Cookie / TechnologyTypePurposeDuration
Session state (browser-managed)Strictly necessaryMaintains basic website functionality during your visitSession only
Google FontsFunctionalDelivers the Inter and Playfair Display typefaces used on this site. Google may log font requests. See Google's privacy policy for details.Up to 1 year

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Hotjar, or any similar behavioral analytics or advertising technology.

Third-Party Technologies

Google Fonts is loaded from Google's servers to deliver our typography. This results in a request to Google's CDN when you visit our site, which may log your IP address. You can prevent this by blocking Google's CDN in your browser settings or using a browser extension that blocks font requests.

If we add any additional third-party technologies that set cookies in the future, this policy will be updated before those services are enabled.

Managing Cookies

Because we use only strictly necessary and functional cookies and do not use tracking or advertising cookies, no cookie consent banner is shown on this site. If you wish to block all cookies, you can do so through your browser settings. Instructions are available for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari. Blocking all cookies may affect website functionality.

Changes to This Policy

If we add new technologies that use cookies, we will update this policy before enabling them. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to info@pacificpoint.co.
