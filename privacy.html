Privacy Policy

How Pacific Point Partners collects, uses, and protects personal information.

Last Updated: 05/01/2026  ·  This policy applies to pacificpoint.co and all Pacific Point Partners advisory services.

1. Who We Are

Pacific Point Partners ("we", "us", "our") is an international advisory firm providing corporate services, market entry advisory, and regulatory support across Vietnam and Asia. For questions about this policy, contact us at info@pacificpoint.co.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

We collect personal information when you submit an inquiry through our contact form, request the VIFC Guide, or subscribe to our regulatory intelligence briefing. This includes your name, email address, organization name, and any details you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

Our website collects standard server log data including IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is retained for security purposes and is not used for behavioral profiling or cross-site tracking. We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any similar technology.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, deliver the VIFC Guide or regulatory briefing you requested, and send our monthly regulatory intelligence briefing to subscribers. Every briefing includes an unsubscribe link. We maintain records of communications as required by applicable professional standards. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a lawful basis, we rely on legitimate interests for responding to inquiries and maintaining business records, and on consent for sending our regulatory intelligence briefing. You may withdraw consent for the briefing at any time by unsubscribing.

5. Data Retention

We retain inquiry and contact information for up to three years from the date of last communication, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or we have an ongoing advisory relationship. Subscriber email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, or to receive a copy in portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@pacificpoint.co. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. We do not store payment card information.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This results in a request to Google's servers when you visit our site. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or any similar analytics or advertising technology. If you submit a contact form, your submission is processed by Formspree under their privacy policy. We do not use any other third-party data processors for website visitors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website following any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to info@pacificpoint.co.
