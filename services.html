VIFC Market Entry

The Vietnam International Financial Centre represents a significant opportunity for international businesses seeking a well-regulated, strategically located platform for operations in Vietnam. There are two distinct platforms: VIFC Da Nang and VIFC Ho Chi Minh City. They are not interchangeable. Each has different characteristics, eligibility criteria, and operational implications. Pacific Point advises clients on both.

We manage the full entry process. From initial feasibility through to license receipt and operational setup, we handle every stage so our clients can focus on their business.

  • Feasibility assessment and platform selection (Da Nang vs. Ho Chi Minh City)
  • Entity type selection and constitutional drafting
  • Regulatory pre-application meetings and submissions
  • License application management and follow-up
  • Banking and operational setup coordination
  • Post-licensing compliance framework design

Asset Management & Funds

The VIFC is an established platform for asset managers seeking a well-regulated jurisdiction for fund and investment management operations. The structure of a fund determines its investment capacity, investor eligibility, and regulatory burden. Getting it wrong at formation is expensive to fix. Pacific Point works with managers ahead of launch to make sure the structure is right from day one.

We advise on all VIFC-eligible fund types and coordinate with legal counsel and administrators throughout the formation process.

  • Closed-ended and open-ended fund vehicle comparison
  • Private equity, credit, and real asset fund structures
  • Feeder and master fund architecture
  • Offering document review and investor disclosure
  • Subscription document and side letter negotiation support
  • Administrator and auditor selection guidance

How a Pacific Point Engagement Works

We operate on a clear, sequential engagement model. Clients know exactly what is happening and why at every stage.

1

Initial Consultation

A complimentary 30-minute call with a senior advisor. We want to understand your objectives, your current structure, and your timeline. No obligation. No sales process. Just a direct conversation about whether Pacific Point is the right fit for what you are trying to do.

2

Scoping & Feasibility

We conduct a detailed assessment of your requirements and prepare a written scope of engagement covering timeline, deliverables, and fees. This document governs the entire engagement.

3

Structured Execution

Your assigned senior advisor leads all regulatory interactions, document preparation, and third-party coordination. Progress is reported on a structured schedule. Weekly during active phases. Monthly during steady-state periods.

4

Completion & Handover

At mandate completion, we deliver a comprehensive handover file covering all regulatory correspondence, corporate documents, and a compliance roadmap for the 12 months ahead.

5

Ongoing Relationship

Most clients retain Pacific Point on a standing advisory basis following their initial engagement. This covers regulatory monitoring, compliance advisory, and ad hoc support under a fixed monthly arrangement.
